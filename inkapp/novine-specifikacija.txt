NOVINE - APP SPECIFICATION (X4 Pro, 480x800 portrait)

An e-paper news reader called "Novine" (Croatian: "Newspaper").
All on-screen text must be in Croatian exactly as written in quotes below.

==================================================================
1. DATA
==================================================================
URL: https://zsimunovic.github.io/xteink-novine/najnovije.txt
- GET request, append "?v=<epoch seconds>" (cache buster), timeout 30 s.
- Keep Wi-Fi up for 3 minutes while the app is open; release it when the app is left.
- Download automatically on open ONLY if there is no saved edition, or the saved
  edition's date is older than today's date on the device. "Osvjezi" button forces it.
- Save the response body to app storage as "izdanje.txt". Always draw from the saved
  copy so the app works offline. If the new download is invalid, keep the old copy.

File format: UTF-8, one record per line, TAG|value
  D  date YYYY-MM-DD (required; reject the file without it)
  N  edition title
  R  period covered
  V  weather for Zagreb (one sentence)
  G  generated timestamp
  U  one "Ukratko" headline (repeats, usually 5)
  S  start of a section; value = section name
  T  article title      (belongs to the latest S)
  X  article text       (belongs to the latest T)
  Z  "why it matters"   (optional)
  I  sources            (optional)
  K  closing quote / fun fact (optional)
  A  author of K        (optional)
Ignore unknown tags.

Example:
  D|2026-11-27
  N|Jutarnji pregled
  V|Zagreb danas: 1-5 °C, oblačno.
  U|Prva kratka vijest.
  S|Hrvatska
  T|Naslov članka
  X|Tekst članka u jednom retku.
  Z|Zašto je to važno.
  I|N1, Index
  K|Zanimljivost za kraj.

Replace characters the font may lack: en/em dash -> "-", « » „ “ ” -> ",
‘ ’ -> ', … -> ..., non-breaking space -> space. Keep č ć đ š ž ° €.

==================================================================
2. HOME SCREEN
==================================================================
- Edition title (large font), below it the date in Croatian, e.g.
  "petak, 27. studenoga 2026." then a horizontal line.
  Weekdays: nedjelja ponedjeljak utorak srijeda četvrtak petak subota
  Months (genitive): siječnja veljače ožujka travnja svibnja lipnja srpnja
  kolovoza rujna listopada studenoga prosinca
- Framed box with the weather sentence (small font, max 4 lines).
- If the saved edition is older than today, a small line:
  "Ovo je izdanje od 27.11. - dodirni Osvježi."
- Tappable rows, 68 px high, thin line under each, ">" at the right end:
  "Ukratko" with "5 vijesti" on the right,
  one row per section with "<n> članaka" on the right,
  "Za kraj" (only if K exists).
- Status line above the buttons, e.g. "Preuzimam današnje novine...",
  "Preuzeto u 07:05.", "Nema veze. Prikazujem spremljeno izdanje."
- No edition at all: "Još nema preuzetog izdanja. Uključi Wi-Fi i dodirni Osvježi."
- Two framed buttons at the bottom: "Osvježi" and "Zaklj. ekran: da" / "Zaklj. ekran: ne"
  (toggles this app as the lock screen).

==================================================================
3. READING SCREEN (one per section)
==================================================================
- Header: framed "< Natrag" button, section name (large), page "2/5" at the right,
  line below.
- Text is word-wrapped on spaces and split into pages:
  article title = large, article text = medium,
  "Zašto je važno: ..." = small, slightly indented,
  "Izvor: ..." = small, short centered line between articles.
- "Ukratko" section: the weather sentence first, then each headline as "- text"
  with a hanging indent.
- "Za kraj": the K text, then "- <author>" indented to the right.
- Bottom buttons: "< Prethodna" and "Sljedeća >". On the last page of a section the
  right button reads "Dalje: <next section>" (or "Sljedeća rubrika >" if too long)
  and opens the next section; after the last section return home.
- Tap left third of the body = previous page; elsewhere in the body = next page.
  Swipe left = next, swipe right = previous.
  Side buttons: right/down = next, left/up = previous. Back = home screen.

==================================================================
4. LOCK SCREEN (when enabled)
==================================================================
- "NOVINE" (large) on the left, Croatian date on the right, double line below.
- Weather sentence (small), a line, then the Ukratko headlines as "- text"
  (medium) until the screen is full.
- Footer: "Ostatak u aplikaciji Novine." or, if the saved edition is older than
  today, "Izdanje od 27.11. - otvori Novine za novo."
- Reads only the saved "izdanje.txt"; redraw every hour.

==================================================================
5. STYLE
==================================================================
- Black on white, thin lines and frames, no large filled black areas (ghosting).
- Fonts: small / medium / large only.
- Keep every screen well under 1000 draw commands.
